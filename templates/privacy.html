Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 30, 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you create a Spendly account, we collect basic information such as your name and email address. As you use the Service, we also store the expense data you enter — amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions — so it can be displayed back to you.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used solely to operate the Service: authenticating your account, recording your expenses, and generating summaries and breakdowns of your spending. We do not use your data for advertising, and we do not sell it to third parties.

3. Data Storage

Your account and expense information is stored securely in our database and retained for as long as your account remains active. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly does not share your personal expense data with third parties. Some infrastructure providers (such as hosting services) may process data on our behalf solely to keep the Service running, and are not permitted to use it for their own purposes.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out to us through the contact details provided on your account page.
